About the Show

Fifty Years of Foot Stories

Dr. Frank Santopietro has been asking the same question since 1974: why is this person in pain, and what's actually causing it? Walk Happy brings those conversations out of the exam room — covering biomechanics, orthotics, sports medicine, footwear, and the surprisingly complex connection between your feet and the rest of your body.

🦶
Biomechanics, Explained Simply
Why flat feet cause hip pain, how gait patterns change over a lifetime, and what most people never learn about how their feet actually work.
🏈
Stories from the Sideline
25 years as team podiatrist for the New England Patriots. What elite athletes have in common with your neighbor who can't walk to the mailbox without pain.
👟
Footwear Deep Dives
Why the shoe you buy at a running store may be making your problem worse — and what to actually look for when choosing footwear for your foot type.
🔬
The Science of Custom Orthotics
How the EvenKeel patented orthotic system was developed, what separates a real custom orthotic from a $40 insert, and what the research actually says.
👶
Kids and Foot Development
When to worry about a child's gait, what "growing pains" actually means, and why early intervention changes outcomes dramatically.
🤝
Practitioner Perspectives
Conversations with physical therapists, orthopedic surgeons, and sports medicine doctors on how foot mechanics intersect with their specialties.
